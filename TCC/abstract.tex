\begin{foreignabstract}

This work presents a structured approach for symbolic modeling and simulation of control strategies applied to underwater manipulators with six degrees of freedom. Based on the Lagrangian formulation, the dynamic model incorporates hydrodynamic effects such as added mass, drag, and buoyancy. The model was implemented in MATLAB/Simulink to analyze the performance of classical and modern controllers, including Computed Torque Control, LQR, PID, and Model Predictive Control (MPC). Simulation results under different operational conditions highlight the comparative performance and robustness of each strategy. The proposed methodology demonstrates how detailed symbolic modeling can be effectively integrated with advanced control techniques, providing a solid foundation for future applications in complex underwater environments.

\textbf{Keywords:} Underwater Manipulators. Predictive Control. Symbolic Modeling. Lagrangian Dynamics. Simulink Simulation.

\end{foreignabstract}
